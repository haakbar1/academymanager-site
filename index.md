# Academy Director

The complete operations toolkit for a sports academy — branches, courts, coaches, memberships, scheduling, attendance, inventory, costs, and reports — synced across every device your team uses.

## What you can do

- **Run multiple branches** with their own courts, coaches, and schedules.
- **Manage memberships** for group, private, and other formats — pick the days, hours, capacity, and price; the app builds the weekly schedule automatically.
- **Track members** including ages, contact info, attendance, absences, cancellations, and notes.
- **Generate PDF reports** for cost, income, profit, branch hours, coach hours, customers, and any day / week / month schedule — branded with your academy logo as a watermark.
- **See the right view per role.** Managers see everything; Co-Managers match the Manager view; Administrators handle day-to-day; Coaches see only their own confirmed sessions.

## Multi-device sync

Sign in on every iPhone or iPad your team uses. Every change a manager makes appears on the coach's phone within ~1 second, and vice versa. Staff join your academy with a one-tap invite code you generate inside Settings — no spreadsheets, no exports, no IT.

## Privacy first

Your academy data lives in your own private workspace. No analytics SDKs, no ads, no tracking. Full details in the [Privacy Policy](privacy.html).
