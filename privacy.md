# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-05-01
**App:** Academy Manager (iOS)

This privacy policy describes how the Academy Manager app handles information. Academy Manager is a local-first app: it does not have a backend server, and the developer does not receive, see, or store any data you put into the app.

## What data the app collects

**None is sent off your device.** Specifically:

- We do not operate any server that receives data from the app.
- We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs.
- We do not collect device identifiers, IP addresses, contacts, location, or any usage analytics.

## What data is stored on your device

The app stores the following on your iPhone or iPad, inside its own sandboxed storage, accessible only to you:

- Branches, courts, coaches, memberships, members, attendance records, equipment, costs, scheduled sessions, and reports you generate.
- User accounts you create within the app (Manager / Administrator / Coach), including a salted SHA-256 hash of each password. Plain-text passwords are never stored.
- Your settings (theme, academy name, contact info, notification preferences) and an optional academy logo image you choose.

This data stays on your device. It is included in your device backup if you back up to iCloud or your computer (this is a normal iOS behavior outside our control). If you delete the app, all of this data is removed.

## Photo library access

If you choose to set an academy logo, the app uses the standard iOS photo picker. The picker runs out-of-process under iOS — the app only sees the image you explicitly select, never your full library. The selected image is stored locally with your other settings.

This is why you see the prompt:

> *"Choose an image from your photo library to use as the academy logo."*

You can refuse access; the app will continue to work without a logo.

## Local notifications

The app schedules local notifications on your device to alert you about expiring memberships, low equipment stock, group memberships outside their expected size, and confirmations after manually adding a member in Attendance. These notifications are produced and delivered by your device — they are not sent through any external service. You can turn them off at any time in iOS Settings → Notifications → Academy Manager.

## Reports and sharing

When you generate a PDF or CSV report from inside the app, the file is created and stored locally. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you explicitly tap the iOS share sheet and choose where to send it (email, AirDrop, Files, etc.). The file's destination is then handled by the app you choose to share it with.

## Children's data

Academy Manager is intended for academy operators (managers, administrators, and coaches), not for children. As an operator, you may enter information about members of your academy, including minors. You are responsible for obtaining any necessary consent from members or their guardians to record this information. The app stores it only on your device, as described above.

## Account deletion

You can delete your account at any time inside the app: Settings → Account → **Delete My Account**. This permanently removes your user record from the device and signs you out. If you also want to delete every record in the app (members, schedules, reports, etc.), delete the app from your device — iOS will remove all of its sandboxed data.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app's behavior changes. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above and, where relevant, by an in-app notice.
